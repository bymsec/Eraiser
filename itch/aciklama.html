Erase the scribbles. Open the world.

3D open-world puzzle adventure PC · Steam Coming soon Made solo

You are a tiny eraser in a world made of objects. The last Pencil has scribbled over everything, and you are the one who erases it.

The story

Long ago there was a great war in the forest, but nobody remembers it, because history was literally erased.

After destroying the pencils, the Eraser King regretted it and sacrificed himself to the Balance Structure in the middle of the lake. For years, the Structure erased everything the last surviving Pencil drew.

Now the Structure has stopped, and the Pencil's drawings no longer disappear. Roads are blocked, villages turn on each other. The only one who can erase them is a little eraser kid born from the King's last crumb, who has no idea.

The Pencil didn't start out as a villain. As a child, it watched its family get erased. Now it keeps trying to draw them back, and every attempt comes out as a broken creature.

What makes it different

Erasing has a price

In this world, erasers wear down and shrink every time they erase. So erasing is frowned upon.

You're different

The King's power lets you erase for free. When the villagers notice, they start eyeing you with suspicion.

The Pencil's weakness

It can't scribble on glass and it can't enter water. That's why it keeps away from the Glass Guardians.

Strike or spare

At some point you face a broken creature. Whether you strike is up to you. Then you find out who it was.

How it plays

1

Explore

Roam the island, talk to the villagers, remember the places you can't reach yet.

2

Solve

Puzzles built from buttons, cables, pressure plates, jump pads, boxes and stones.

3

Earn

Main puzzles grant permanent abilities. Hidden chests give coins.

4

Return

Use your new ability to open places you couldn't get through before.

No arrows, no markers, no map pins. You usually see a puzzle's reward and its obstacle at the same time. Curiosity does the rest.

The world

The world is made of islands, and every island is its own kingdom. Sail between them whenever you like.

Forest Kingdom

The starting island: the main village, waterfall cliffs, Number Village, the harbor, the Tech Village inside a glass dome, and the first dungeon.

Ice Kingdom

Next up. Frozen forests and scribbles trapped under ice.

Lava Kingdom

Rivers of lava and trees with ember leaves. You can see it on the horizon from the forest.

Say hello to the locals

Everyone here is an object. Faces are just two dots and a line; personality comes from voices, moves and words. 60+ characters live on the forest island alone.
